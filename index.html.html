Questions? We got you

How long does the achaar last?

Keep it in a cool place, sealed. It stays fresh for weeks. For best taste, use a clean spoon every time and keep the lid tight. If you buy bulk, ask us for storage tips on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver to my area?

We deliver to homes, shops and private sellers. Send us your address on WhatsApp and we will confirm if we deliver to you, the fee, and how fast we can get there.

What is the minimum order?

No minimum stress — you can start with just one 250ml for R10. Bulk buyers and resellers usually go for 10L or 20L for better value.

Can I resell Limpopo Fresh Achaar?

Yes! Many of our customers are private sellers. Send “I want to resell” on WhatsApp and we will send bulk prices and tips to start selling in your area.

Is it very hot?

You choose: Mild for soft heat, Medium for proper kasi balance, Hot for serious fire. Medium is the most popular — if you are not sure, start there.

How do I pay?

We confirm your total + delivery on WhatsApp first, then confirm payment with you. No payment is taken on this website — everything is confirmed on WhatsApp.
